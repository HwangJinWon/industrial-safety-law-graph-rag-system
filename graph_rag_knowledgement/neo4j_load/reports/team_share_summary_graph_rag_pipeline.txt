Team Share Summary - Graph-RAG Pipeline

- Source hierarchy loaded.
- ListItem -> ITEM_RESOLVES_TO -> source anchor links loaded.

What currently works:

  - mode

- /ask also accepts query/message aliases.
- /ask returns:
  - answer
  - evidences[] legacy alias
  - citations[]
  - evidence_chips[]

  - evidence_readiness
  - answer_policy

  - missing_evidence
