Rendering changes
-----------------
- openLaw() now preserves detail.penalties as selectedLaw.penalties[] instead of only joined text.
- openLaw() now preserves detail.candidate_penalties as selectedLaw.candidatePenalties[] instead of joining candidates into one long string.
- selectedLaw.penalty remains as a legacy/fallback string, but rendering now prioritizes structured penalties[] and candidatePenalties[].

Penalty display separation
--------------------------
- Direct penalties are shown under "직접 연결된 벌칙·과태료 근거" style content with a direct-linked badge.
- Candidate penalties are shown under "관련 가능성이 있는 벌칙·과태료 조항 후보" as separate cards.
- Candidate cards show law_name/article_no, preview/text, match_type, confidence, match_reason, and candidate_notice.
- Candidate cards use warning/info styling and show "후보", "검토 필요", and "확정 아님" badges.
- When no direct or candidate penalty exists, the panel shows a single conservative no-direct-penalty message.

User-facing wording
-------------------
- The detail panel now favors "연결된 근거" and "관련 근거" language rather than developer-oriented Graph-RAG wording.
- Developer node id remains behind the existing details disclosure.

Remaining limitation
--------------------
- Candidate penalties are still article-reference review candidates, not final violation-to-penalty conclusions.
- Frontend display is a minimal structured improvement, not a full penalty reasoning UI.
