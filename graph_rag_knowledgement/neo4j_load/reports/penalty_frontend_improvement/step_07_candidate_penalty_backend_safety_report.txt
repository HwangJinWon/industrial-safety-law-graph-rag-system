- 05_code/graph_rag_pipeline/ask_adapter.py

Separation rule
---------------
- penalties[] is reserved for direct graph-linked or direct penalty rule evidence and is_confirmed=true.
- candidate_penalties[] is reserved for article-reference matches and is_confirmed=false.
- Article-reference matching is not promoted to confirmed penalty evidence.

Candidate schema
----------------
Each candidate_penalties[] item now includes:
- law_name
- article_no
- title / citation_text
- text
- preview
- match_type
- confidence
- match_reason
- candidate_notice
- source_node_id or rule_id when available
- is_confirmed=false

Direct penalty schema
---------------------
Each penalties[] item includes:
- law_name
- article_no
- text
- preview
- match_type=DIRECT_LINKED or direct-linked equivalent in response context
- confidence=high
- source_node_id or rule_id when available
- is_confirmed=true

Candidate limit and dedupe
--------------------------
- candidate_penalties[] is limited to 5 items in /law/{nodeId} responses.
- Duplicate candidates are removed by rule_id + article_no + preview + match_type.

Warning behavior
----------------
- If direct penalties are absent and candidate penalties exist, both a direct-penalty-not-found warning and a candidate-review-required warning are returned.
- If direct penalties and candidates both exist, they remain separated and candidates are treated as supplemental review material only.
- If no direct or candidate penalty exists, only direct-penalty-not-found is returned.
