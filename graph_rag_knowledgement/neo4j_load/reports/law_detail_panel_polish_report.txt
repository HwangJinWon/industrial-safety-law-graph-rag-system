What changed
------------
1. Duplicate cleanup
   - /law/{nodeId} now deduplicates requirements, warnings, penalties, citations, and related_sources.
   - Requirements that repeat the same text as source_text/preview are removed.
   - Direct-penalty warning is returned once.

2. Summary improvement
   - summary is no longer copied directly from source_text.
   - A short rule-based summary is generated by node type:
     - LogicalRow/LogicalCell: table or annex row/cell evidence.
     - Article/Paragraph/Item/Subitem: law text/context evidence.
     - Rule: obligation/requirement evidence.
     - SourceNode fallback: source-unit evidence.

3. why_relevant improvement
   - Developer-facing wording such as "Graph-RAG evidence lookup" was replaced with user-facing text:
     "This item is connected to evidence used to check safety and health standards related to the entered site conditions."

4. Internal ID display
   - Frontend no longer shows internal IDs such as LogicalRow:lrow_* as primary citation text.
   - Internal node_id is moved to a small collapsible developer detail area.
   - The main detail panel prioritizes citation_text, law_name, article_no, and annex_title.

5. Requirements display
   - Requirements are displayed as short bullet previews.
   - Long entries are truncated around 220 characters.
   - If no distinct structured requirements exist, the panel says that no separate requirement structure was found in the linked evidence.

6. Penalty/warning display
   - The no-direct-penalty warning is shown once.
   - Message changed to:
     "The currently linked evidence does not identify a direct penalty provision. Penalty exposure should be reviewed together with separate penalty or administrative fine provisions."

7. Frontend detail panel polish
   - Section wording changed from "Graph-RAG linked provision" to "linked evidence/source".
   - Detail panel order is now:
     related law, law/article/annex metadata, summary, source text/preview, why relevant, linked source, requirements, penalty, warnings, developer node id.
